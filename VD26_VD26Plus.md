# VD26 and VD26+ Display
## Features
__VD26__

* Power on/off control: Press and hold the button for 3 seconds to turn the display on or off.
* Mode selection: Single press the button to switch between two modes: ECO and SPORT.
* Lighting control: Press and hold the button for 3 seconds to turn the light on/off.
* Auto power-off: The display automatically turns off after 3 minutes of inactivity (when the bike is not moving).

__VD26+__
> Includes all features of the VD26.
* Additional feature: Displays real-time speed (in km/h).
